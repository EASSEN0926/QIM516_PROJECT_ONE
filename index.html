Healthy Food Adventure

Help Lina learn how to choose foods that give energy, support growth, and protect the body.

Story Intro

A short narration video introduces Lina's problem and the healthy food mission.

Hi! I am Lina. I love candy and soda, but sometimes I feel tired in class. Can you help me choose foods that make my body strong?

Narration goal: introduce the problem, connect food with energy, and invite children to complete healthy food missions.

Learn Food Groups

Click each food group. Children learn before playing, so the games have clear educational meaning.

👆Click a food group to see a child-friendly explanation.

Game 1: Healthy Plate Builder

Goal: children learn that a balanced meal needs different food groups.

Choose fruit,
vegetables,
protein and grains.
Build a balanced plate using 4 useful foods.

Game 2: Feed Lina Mission

Goal: apply nutrition knowledge in a school morning situation.

Lina has school this morning. What should she eat?

Choose one breakfast for Lina.

Game 3: Food Function Sorting

Goal: classify food by function: energy, growth, and protection.

Click to Sort

💪 Grow Food

Sort all foods into their body function groups.

Game 4: Memory Match

Goal: strengthen memory through retrieval practice.

Open two cards. Try to match food and group.

Final Quiz

Goal: check understanding and provide immediate feedback.
